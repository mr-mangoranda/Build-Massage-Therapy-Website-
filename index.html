The Beauty of Maranao

Okir Art (Ukkil)

This is the traditional Maranao art style using flowing, plant-inspired patterns. You see it in torogan houses, wood carvings, textiles, and metal crafts. Okir represents life, unity, and nobility.

Torogan (Royal House)

The torogan is the ancestral house of Maranao royalty (datu or sultan). It symbolizes power, leadership, and social status, and is decorated with okir designs, especially the panolong beams.

Darangen Epic

The Darangen is a long heroic epic chant that tells stories of Maranao ancestors and heroes like Bantugan. It preserves Maranao values such as honor, bravery, respect, and wisdom, and is recognized by UNESCO.

Kandori (Thanksgiving)

A kandori is a ceremonial feast held for important events like healing, weddings, or thanksgiving. It strengthens community ties, spirituality, and respect for elders and traditions.

Malong (Garment)

The malong is a versatile tube cloth used as clothing, blanket, carrier, or ceremonial wear. It reflects adaptability, creativity, and daily Maranao life.
